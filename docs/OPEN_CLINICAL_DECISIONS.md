# Open clinical and regulatory decisions

Do not guess. When a rule depends on an external standard or facility policy
that is not available in this repository, implement the control as configurable
and record the question here.

These items are labeled **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION**.
This software is compliance-supporting and validation-ready. It is not
automatically FDA, AABB, CAP, or CLIA certified.

| OCD-021 | Antibody ID supervisor review | Must every identification be reviewed by a distinct supervisor before posting history? | Defaults on: `AntibodyId.RequireSupervisorReview` and `AntibodyId.BlockSelfReview`. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-023 | Dual antibody-identification posting paths | Should completing a reviewed workup replace free-text ABID result verification as the only history-posting path? | Default: the workup is the identification of record when it is in scope. An open workup (same specimen, linked ABID result, or unscoped) HardStops free-text ABID verify that would post history, and a blood-attribute antibody verify that would post or deactivate history (`ABID-WORKUP-OPEN`). Patient-chart Add antibody or Deactivate has no specimen, so any open workup on that patient HardStops chart add and deactivate (`ABID-WORKUP-OPEN`) until complete or void. Deactivate after complete or void remains the authorized immuno path (OCD-017); deactivating a specificity posted by a completed workup warns (`ABID-DEACT-POSTED`) and still deactivates. A completed workup on the same specimen or linked result skips free-text or blood-attribute antibody history changes (`ABID-WORKUP-AUTHORITATIVE`) and warns if the verified result disagrees (`ABID-WORKUP-DISAGREE`). Rejecting a linked specimen warns (`ABID-SPEC-OPEN`), withdraws interpretation and review, and treats the still-open workup as the identification of record for the patient until it is voided or linked to a usable specimen. Opening another workup while that rejected or cancelled link remains HardStops (`ABID-WORKUP-DUP-OPEN`); re-linking the same workup to a usable specimen is allowed. A clock-expired or expired-status linked specimen is also patient-wide for history post and new workups; complete remains allowed with acknowledgment (OCD-025). Editing collection or expiration warns (`ABID-SPEC-EDIT`) and withdraws interpretation. Labs with no workup still post from verified free-text ABID. Electronic XM HardStops while any workup is open on that patient (`XM-EC-ABID-OPEN`); serologic XM is still available and warns (`ABID-XM-OPEN`). Allocation warns (`ABID-ALLOC-OPEN`) and still reserves. Issue warns (`ABID-ISSUE-OPEN`) after the gate and still issues without requiring override. Completing while reserved or issued units remain warns (`ABID-COMPLETE-PRODUCTS`) and requires acknowledgment; it does not identify antibodies or release those units. Voiding while reserved or issued units remain warns (`ABID-VOID-PRODUCTS`) and still voids. Manually changing ABO/Rh, or verifying an ABO/Rh result that changes current type, while a workup is open warns (`ABID-ABO-OPEN`) and withdraws interpretation and review. Correcting a verified ABO/Rh or antigen result while a workup is open warns (`ABID-TYPE-CORR`) and does not withdraw; complete and Accept HardStop (`ABID-TYPE-PENDING`) until verify or invalidate (OCD-029). Changing type after a completed in-scope workup warns (`ABID-AG-DONE` / `ABID-ABO-DONE`) and does not reopen or retract (OCD-030). Adding or deactivating a special requirement while a workup is open warns (`ABID-SR-OPEN`) and still records. Workup complete still posts only technologist-Identified findings after review, skipping an existing catalog-id or ordinal specificity match. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |
| OCD-024 | Antigen method vs predicted genotype | Which antigen-profile Method values are predicted genotype vs serologic phenotype? | Default tokens (case-insensitive substring): genotype, molecular, predicted, dna, pcr. Other/blank methods are treated as phenotype (`ABID-PHENO-CONFLICT` / `ABID-INTERP-PHENO`). Comparison is advisory and does not HardStop identification. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-025 | Antibody ID on an expired specimen | May staff complete a workup after the linked specimen clock or status expires? | Default: create/link HardStops rejected, cancelled, expired-status, or clock-expired specimens (`ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-UNUSABLE` / `ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-EXPIRED`). Complete HardStops rejected or cancelled. Complete on a clock-expired or expired-status specimen is a Warning that requires acknowledgment (`ABID-COMPLETE-ACK`). Completing still posts only technologist-Identified antibodies. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |
| OCD-026 | Antibody ID before specimen accept | May staff open or complete a workup on a Collected or Received (not yet Accepted) specimen? | Default: Collected HardStops create, link, review Accept, and complete (`ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-NOT-READY`). Received warns at create/link (`ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-UNACCEPTED`) and requires acknowledgment at Accept/complete. Completing still posts only technologist-Identified antibodies. An open Received linked specimen is also patient-wide for history post and new workups (OCD-031). An open Collected linked specimen is likewise patient-wide (OCD-032). **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |
| OCD-027 | Antibody ID after panel lot expires | May staff complete a workup after an attached panel lot expires or is deactivated? | Default: create and attach still HardStop expired or inactive lots (`ABID-LOT-EXPIRED` / `ABID-LOT-INACTIVE`). On an open workup, a deactivated lot HardStops review Accept and complete. Clock expiry on an attached lot warns and requires acknowledgment (`ABID-COMPLETE-ACK` / `ABID-REVIEW-ACK`). | OCD-028 | Antibody ID vs assist exclusion rationale | Must a technologist document why they identify a specificity that assistance would exclude? | Default: interpret HardStops (`ABID-INTERP-EXCL-REASON`) until a rationale is recorded on those Identified items. The identification itself remains a Warning (`ABID-INTERP-EXCLUDED`, OCD-020). Assistance does not identify antibodies. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-029 | Antibody ID during a pending type correction | May staff complete identification while an ABO/Rh or antigen correction is entered but not yet verified? | Default: no. Correct warns (`ABID-TYPE-CORR`) and does not withdraw, because stored type has not changed. Complete and supervisor Accept HardStop (`ABID-TYPE-PENDING`) until the correction is verified or invalidated. A scoped workup HardStops only for a pending correction on the linked specimen; an unscoped workup HardStops for any current patient type correction. The open worklist flags those rows (`HasPendingTypeCorrection`) and filters `/antibody-id?typePending=1`. Verify of a type-changing correction still warns (`ABID-AG-OPEN` / `ABID-ABO-OPEN`) and withdraws. Invalidate of the correction restores the prior verified row (OCD-016) and may withdraw (`ABID-AG-INVAL` / `ABID-ABO-INVAL`). **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |
| OCD-030 | Type change after completed antibody ID | Must changing ABO/Rh or antigen type after a completed workup reopen identification or retract posted history? | Default: no. An in-scope type change warns (`ABID-AG-DONE` / `ABID-ABO-DONE`) and still records. Verify uses the same-specimen completed workup (`AppliesToCompletedWorkup`). Chart save warns for a completed unscoped workup, or when a posted Identified specificity conflicts with a new antigen-positive type (that antigen only). Verify on another specimen warns on the same posted conflict. Posted history is not auto-retracted (OCD-017); phenotype stays in place (OCD-022). **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |
| OCD-031 | Antibody ID on a received-not-accepted specimen | Must a Received (not yet Accepted) linked specimen keep exclusive specimen scope? | Default: no. `IsPatientWideIdentificationScope` includes Received. An open workup on that draw is the identification of record for the patient until the specimen is Accepted, the workup is voided, completed with acknowledgment, or re-linked. Other-specimen history post and a second workup HardStop (`ABID-WORKUP-OPEN` / `ABID-WORKUP-DUP-OPEN`). Complete and Accept remain Warning + acknowledgment (`ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-UNACCEPTED`, OCD-026). This does not identify antibodies. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |
| OCD-032 | Antibody ID on a collected-not-received specimen | Must a Collected (not yet received) linked specimen keep exclusive specimen scope? | Default: no. `IsPatientWideIdentificationScope` includes Collected. An open workup on that draw is the identification of record for the patient until the specimen is received, the workup is voided, or re-linked. Other-specimen history post and a second workup HardStop (`ABID-WORKUP-OPEN` / `ABID-WORKUP-DUP-OPEN`). Create, link, complete, and Accept remain HardStop (`ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-NOT-READY`, OCD-026). This does not identify antibodies. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |

The continuous-improvement loop must ask these questions in the active
conversation when the next ranked residual depends on them. Do not invent

When a decision is closed, move the row to the bottom with the effective date,
the chosen configuration values, and the validation evidence ID. Do not delete
the history.
