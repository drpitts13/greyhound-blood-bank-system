# Open clinical and regulatory decisions

Do not guess. When a rule depends on an external standard or facility policy
that is not available in this repository, implement the control as configurable
and record the question here.

These items are labeled **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION**.
This software is compliance-supporting and validation-ready. It is not
automatically FDA, AABB, CAP, or CLIA certified.

| ID | Topic | Question | Current software behavior | Suggested owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OCD-001 | Electronic XM after undetectable antibody | Does a historically identified, currently undetectable antibody permanently preclude computer XM? | Any antibody-history row (active or deactivated) blocks electronic XM (`XM-EC-HISTORY`). Antigen-negative requirements still evaluate all history rows. | Transfusion medicine medical director |
| OCD-002 | Specimen validity windows | Confirm alloimmunization-risk vs standard specimen windows and lookback days against current AABB language and facility SOP. | Defaults: 72 hours when recent transfusion/pregnancy; 168 hours otherwise; 90-day lookback. Facility policy keys exist. | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-003 | Uncrossmatched ABO/Rh | Confirm whether uncrossmatched RBC/WB must be group O, and whether childbearing-age Rh-negative recipients require O-negative, including age cutoff. | Defaults on (`UncrossmatchedCellularMustBeGroupO`, `UncrossmatchedONegForChildbearing`, age 50). Emergency path is Warning + override, not "ignore all rules." | Medical director |
| OCD-004 | ISBT 128 licensed tables | Confirm DIN check, ABO/RhD codes, product codes, and data structures against current ICCBBA documentation. | Parsers and placeholder catalogs are present. Seeded codes are not a licensed ICCBBA extract. | Interface / ISBT administrator + ICCBBA licensee |
| OCD-005 | Receipt temperature range | Confirm 1–10 °C (and frozen-product ranges) against current product circulars and facility SOP. | Default receive-temperature HardStop uses 1–10 °C when the policy is enabled. | Inventory supervisor |
| OCD-006 | Computer XM AABB 5.16 validation | When may the facility enable electronic XM in production? | `AllowElectronicCrossmatch` is a facility policy flag. Disabled policy is a HardStop (`XM-EC-POLICY`). | Quality + medical director |
| OCD-007 | Record retention | Confirm retention years against current 21 CFR 606.160 interpretation and state law. | Default retention metadata is 10 years; clinical/audit purge is not implemented. | Quality / compliance |
| OCD-008 | Second-verifier policy | Which workflows require a distinct directory second user vs validated electronic identification? | Configurable flags exist for issue, receive, discard, quarantine release, directed conversion, and transfusion dual ID. | Medical director + nursing |
| OCD-009 | Inactive patient clinical use | May an Inactive (not Merged) patient receive testing, allocation, or issue? | Merged records are HardStop `PAT-MERGED-INACTIVE`. Inactive records remain usable. | Medical director + registration |
| OCD-010 | Manual merge authorization | Does merging two patient records require a second authorizer in addition to `patient.merge`? | Manual merge requires `patient.merge` (not `patient.write`). Seeded to Supervisor and Administrator, not Technologist. Reason is required. Discordant ABO/Rh remains a HardStop. A second authorizer is not implemented. | Medical director + registration + quality |
| OCD-011 | Unit retype self-verify | May the same user who entered a unit ABO/Rh retype verify it and release the unit? | Default `Inventory.BlockRetypeSelfVerify` is on. Record leaves the unit Received. Verify applies `RES-SELF-VERIFY` and only then moves to Available or Quarantine. | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-012 | MTP vs emergency privilege | Should massive transfusion use a distinct privilege from emergency release? | Both require `issue.emergency-release`. Seeded to Supervisor and Administrator, not Technologist. | Medical director + blood bank supervisor |
| OCD-013 | Patient ABO/Rh self-verify | May the same user who entered a patient ABO/Rh verify it and establish the current type? | Default `Result.BlockAboSelfVerify` is on. Save/complete leaves the result Entered. Verify applies `RES-SELF-VERIFY` and only then writes current `PatientBloodTypeHistory`. | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-014 | Lookback vs inventory recall privilege | Must a lookback recall also require `inventory.recall`, or is `lookback.manage` sufficient? | DIN recall requires `lookback.manage` and calls `RecallForLookbackAsync`. Direct inventory recall requires `inventory.recall` (`INV-RCL-PERM`). | Medical director + inventory supervisor + quality |
| OCD-015 | Directed conversion vs quarantine-release privilege | Should converting a directed unit to allogeneic inventory use a distinct privilege from quarantine release? | Both require `inventory.release` in the Application service (`INV-DIR-PERM` / `INV-REL-PERM`). | Medical director + inventory supervisor + quality |
| OCD-016 | Restore prior verified after invalidating a correction | When an unverified correction is invalidated, should the prior verified version become current again? | Default: yes. The correction is marked Invalidated and `SupersededByResultId` is cleared on the prior verified row. | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-017 | History after result invalidation | Must invalidating a verified ABO/Rh or antibody-identification result automatically retract posted `PatientBloodTypeHistory` / `AntibodyHistory`? | Default: no automatic revert. The result row is superseded; history remains until an authorized immuno/correction path changes it. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Medical director + quality |
| OCD-018 | Instrument/interface auto-pending | Must instrument- and interface-derived results always wait for a distinct verification step? | Default: yes. `ResultSource.Instrument` and `Interface` start `PendingVerification`. Manual/calculated start `Entered`. | Blood bank supervisor |
| OCD-019 | Inbound ORU OBX-11 codes | Which HL7 observation result statuses may post a pending result versus NAK? | Default: empty, F (final), C (correction of unreleased), P (preliminary), and R (results entered — not verified) post. X/D/I/W and unknown codes NAK (`RES-IFACE-OBX-STATUS`). **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Interface administrator + blood bank supervisor |
| OCD-022 | Antigen phenotype versioning | Must changing a patient antigen phenotype create a new history row instead of updating in place? | Default: no. The row is updated in place and the prior Result/Method are kept on the `Antibody` audit old value. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-020 | Antibody ID exclusion thresholds | How many homozygous vs heterozygous cells are required to rule out, and which antigens show dosage? | Defaults: dosage-aware on; 1 homozygous exclusion for dosage-sensitive antigens (C,c,E,e,FYA,FYB,JKA,JKB,M,N,S,s); 2 antigen-positive negatives when dosage-aware is off. Assistance only. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-021 | Antibody ID supervisor review | Must every identification be reviewed by a distinct supervisor before posting history? | Defaults on: `AntibodyId.RequireSupervisorReview` and `AntibodyId.BlockSelfReview`. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-023 | Dual antibody-identification posting paths | Should completing a reviewed workup replace free-text ABID result verification as the only history-posting path? | Default: the workup is the identification of record when it is in scope. An open workup (same specimen, linked ABID result, or unscoped) HardStops free-text ABID verify that would post history (`ABID-WORKUP-OPEN`). A completed workup on the same specimen or linked result skips free-text posting (`ABID-WORKUP-AUTHORITATIVE`) and warns if the text disagrees (`ABID-WORKUP-DISAGREE`). Labs with no workup still post from verified free-text ABID. Workup complete still posts only technologist-Identified findings after review, skipping an existing catalog-id or ordinal specificity match. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |
| OCD-024 | Antigen method vs predicted genotype | Which antigen-profile Method values are predicted genotype vs serologic phenotype? | Default tokens (case-insensitive substring): genotype, molecular, predicted, dna, pcr. Those set `FromGenotype` for assist (`ABID-GENO-CONFLICT`) and interpretation warnings (`ABID-INTERP-GENO`). Other/blank methods are treated as phenotype (`ABID-PHENO-CONFLICT` / `ABID-INTERP-PHENO`). Comparison is advisory and does not HardStop identification. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director |
| OCD-025 | Antibody ID on an expired specimen | May staff complete a workup after the linked specimen clock or status expires? | Default: create/link HardStops rejected, cancelled, expired-status, or clock-expired specimens (`ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-UNUSABLE` / `ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-EXPIRED`). Complete HardStops rejected or cancelled. Complete on a clock-expired or expired-status specimen is a Warning that requires acknowledgment (`ABID-COMPLETE-ACK`). Completing still posts only technologist-Identified antibodies. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |
| OCD-026 | Antibody ID before specimen accept | May staff open or complete a workup on a Collected or Received (not yet Accepted) specimen? | Default: Collected HardStops create, link, review Accept, and complete (`ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-NOT-READY`). Received warns at create/link (`ABID-WORKUP-SPEC-UNACCEPTED`) and requires acknowledgment at Accept/complete. Completing still posts only technologist-Identified antibodies. **REQUIRES REGULATORY / SME VERIFICATION** | Blood bank supervisor + medical director + quality |

When a decision is closed, move the row to the bottom with the effective date,
the chosen configuration values, and the validation evidence ID. Do not delete
the history.
