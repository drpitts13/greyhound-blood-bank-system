![Antibody identification worklist](figures/antibody-id.png)

**Antibody ID** lists workups that are not finished. Filter to the patients or statuses you are responsible for. Open a row to go to that patient's workup.

You need permission to record immunohematology, or permission to override, to see this list. Recording reactions is not the same as identifying an antibody. The workup screen keeps those actions apart. See [Antibody identification](antibody-identification).

A patient with an open workup is marked on the issue desk. You can still issue, with that warning visible. You cannot add or deactivate chart antibodies while the workup is open, and you cannot record an electronic crossmatch for that patient until the workup is no longer open.
