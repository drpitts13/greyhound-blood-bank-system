Greyhound Blood Bank LIS is a hospital transfusion-service laboratory information system. It supports patients, specimens, immunohematology, compatibility, inventory, issue, transfusion documentation, lookback, reactions, and quality deviations.

This Clinical User Guide is for people who do that work: technologists, supervisors, and staff who review billing, interfaces, or the audit trail. Configuration of catalogs, rules, users, and interfaces is in the Administrator Guide.

The system is compliance-supporting. Publishing this guide does not make the software FDA-cleared, AABB-accredited, or CAP-certified. Your facility's procedures, medical director, and validation record decide how you use it.

> Facility policy. Items that depend on a local decision are marked Facility policy. Do not treat those settings as universal rules.

The software is not a blood-collection establishment. It does not determine donor eligibility.

## How to read a topic

Each topic names the screen, what you do there, and what the system will block. **Hard stop** means the action cannot proceed. **Warning** means a supervisor can override it with a reason and, when required, an electronic signature. The screen explains the block in the alert. You do not need a code book to finish the task, but the safety topic lists the checks that come up most often.
