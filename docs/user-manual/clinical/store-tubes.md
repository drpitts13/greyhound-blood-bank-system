**Store tubes** (Patients & testing) records where each tube is kept, so it can be found again for a repeat test, an extra crossmatch, or a transfusion reaction workup.

Scan the rack or type the shelf into **Storage place** and press Enter. Then scan each tube you put there (its accession number or label barcode); each scan stores the tube and waits for the next one. Add a **Note** such as a position if your racks are not labelled by position. The list under the scanner shows what you stored this session, with where each tube was before.

The patient's **Specimens** tab shows each tube's current place under its status, and the tube's history shows every time it was stored or moved. A move never erases the earlier place. Storing a tube where it already is, scanning a tube the system does not know, and storing a cancelled tube are refused with the reason. Each store is in the audit trail.

How long tubes are kept, and when they may be discarded, is your facility's policy (OCD-070); this screen does not discard tubes.

> Storing tubes needs the specimen accession permission.
