**Help** in the sidebar opens this library. You can also open it from the sign-in screen before you have a session, from the link **Help with signing in**.

## Find a topic

Type in **Search help**. The search looks at the title, the short summary, and the body of every topic in both guides. Clear the box to see the full contents again. Topics are grouped the same way as the PDF table of contents.

Open a title to read the topic. **Open this screen** appears when the topic has a fixed address, such as Inventory or Facility Policy. Topics that depend on a patient or a unit do not offer that button. Use **Help for this page** while you are already on that chart or unit.

The previous and next links at the bottom follow the guide order.

## Help for this page

On any signed-in screen, **Help for this page** in the top bar opens the topic that matches the address, including the patient-chart tab you have selected. Unit tabs and the inventory retype and quarantine boards do the same.

## Downloads

**Download clinical guide** and **Download administrator guide** save the PDF manuals. They are generated from these topics, including the figures. Specialized authoring help on Order and Test Rules, Instrument Interfaces, and the SQL report designer stays on those screens and is linked from the administrator topics.

## Dates and times

Times on screen are your workstation's local time, and times you type are local too: the time on the clock, the tube or the paper record. This includes when a specimen was collected and when it expires, an RhIG event and when RhIG was given, a reagent lot's expiry, a delivery's order time and unit expiry, and a new visit's admit time. The system stores them in UTC; you never convert.
