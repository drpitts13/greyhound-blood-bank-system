![Enter units](figures/delivery-receive.png)

This screen books the physical units on a delivery into inventory. Work one unit at a time. Confirm the unit number, product code, ABO and Rh, and expiration against the bag and the packing list. A mismatch is a reason to stop, not a reason to type what the paperwork says if the bag says something else.

Record appearance and temperature when the form asks. Facility policy can require a second person to verify the receipt. That person signs in as the second verifier. You cannot verify your own receipt when the policy requires two people.

Received red cells and whole blood typically wait on the retype board before they are available. Plasma and platelets follow the release rule for that product. If the unit arrives damaged or out of temperature, quarantine or damage it here with the coded reason. Do not put it into available stock and hope someone notices.

Autologous and directed units must be tied to the intended patient at receipt. They will not issue to anyone else. Converting a directed unit to general allogeneic stock is a separate, permissioned action later, with a reason.

After receipt, open the unit chart if you need to print a component label or correct a data entry error. Identity correction (unit number or product code entered wrong) requires the inventory identity-correction permission and a reason. It is not an ordinary edit.
