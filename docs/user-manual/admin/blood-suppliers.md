![Blood suppliers](figures/admin-blood-suppliers.png)

**Blood Suppliers** are the shippers you can select on a delivery. Code, name, and registration are required. Address is optional. A delivery cannot name a supplier who is not on this list, so add the supplier before the first shipment arrives.

Registration is the identifier your procedure uses for that supplier. Do not put a nickname in the registration field and the license number only in a comment. Lookback and receipt reports use the registration.

Inactivate a supplier you no longer receive from. Historical deliveries keep the supplier they were received under.
