![Specimen label](figures/admin-specimen-label.png)

**Specimen Label** is the layout designer for the 2×1 inch specimen label. Drag the patient, specimen, and barcode fields. One layout is active. The next specimen label printed anywhere in the application uses it.

Print a sample to a test printer before you activate a layout. A field that fits on the screen can still collide with the barcode on the printer. The barcode has to scan. If it does not, move the other fields. Do not shrink the barcode below what your scanners read.

This page does not choose the printer. Printers are on [Printers](printers). Technologists pick the printer at print time. They cannot move fields.
